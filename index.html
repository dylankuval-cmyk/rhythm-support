RHYTHM

A daily routine, in rhythm.

Help with your plan, sleep routine and personal wellness logs.

Getting started

Set your schedule during onboarding, then adapt your plan in Protocol. Today brings your next actions together. Rest contains sleep planning and quiet sessions; Cabinet organizes supplements you already use. Progress describes your own entries and completed plans.

Your data

Use Settings to export records or delete local data. Keep exports somewhere you trust. Optional Apple Health, location and notifications can be managed through the app and Apple's system settings. Core manual logging does not require these permissions.

Need help?

Contact: dylankuval@icloud.com. When requesting help, include your app version, iOS version and the steps that caused the issue. Please do not send private health records.

Rhythm is a general wellness tool, not medical advice or a diagnosis or treatment service.

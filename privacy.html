Your rhythm. Your records.

Privacy policy · October 26, 2026

What stays on your device

Rhythm stores your profile, schedules, routines, wellness entries, selected meal photos and session history locally on your device. No account is required. This version has no developer-operated backend, analytics or advertising SDK, and does not transmit wellness records to us.

Optional permissions

If you choose to connect Apple Health, Rhythm requests read access to sleep, steps and workouts. It does not write Health data. Imported records remain local. You can manage Health permissions in Apple's Health or Settings apps.

If you request local daylight estimates, Rhythm obtains your location once, rounds the stored coordinates to approximately a kilometre and calculates sunrise and sunset locally. Location is not sent to a weather service. Notifications are scheduled locally after you allow them. The photo picker shares only photos you select with Rhythm.

Your choices

Settings includes export and deletion controls. Exports go to the destination you choose using the system share sheet; you are responsible for copies you share or save outside Rhythm. Deleting local app data does not delete those external copies. Device backups may contain app data depending on your Apple backup settings; manage those backups with Apple.

External websites and support

Educational links open external websites in your browser. Those sites have their own privacy policies and may receive ordinary browser information; Rhythm does not attach wellness records to those links. If you contact support, the message and any information you choose to include are used to respond. Please avoid sending sensitive health records.

Changes

Future versions may update this policy. New data uses will be described here before they apply.
